Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision

Building the Future of Our Dreams - Creating opportunities for young people to discover their potential and shape their destinies.

Our Mission

To promote the emotional well-being, personal development, social awareness, life-skills and positive engagement of young adults through structured experience.

Welcome to RYAD

We are so excited to have you here! Retreat usually is a special time set apart for introspection and commitment to better 'oneself'. Each of you carries within you unique gifts, talents and ideas that the world is waiting for.

RYAD is about listening and learning, unlearning, re-learning, sharing, connecting and shaping the kind of future you desire for yourself, your families, your communities and even the world.

"The future is not something that just happens to us, it is something we build with the choices we make each day"

You are the leaders, innovators and change-makers of tomorrow. Open your heart and your mind and get ready for a journey of self-discovery.

6 Key Development Areas

01

Psychological Development

Self-awareness, emotional intelligence, self-esteem, resilience, decision-making and positive thinking.

02

Relationships & Communication

Assertive communication, interpersonal relationships, empathy, respect, boundaries and conflict management.

03

Purpose & Personal Development

Goal-setting, delayed gratification, discipline, responsible choices, leadership and personal growth.

04

Digital & Technology Skills

Responsible use of technology, digital citizenship, creativity, innovation and understanding AI opportunities.

05

Healthy & Responsible Living

Education on risky behaviours, addiction prevention, healthy relationships, personal safety and lifestyle choices.

06

Character & Values

Integrity, accountability, respect, compassion, service, faith and responsible citizenship.
